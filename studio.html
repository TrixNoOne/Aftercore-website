one roof, one team

manifesto

we are a small, non-seeded hardware studio. no committee, no roadmap theatre — just parts that get lighter every revision.

AfterCore funds itself with engineering work and spends what is left on the bench: fixtures, alloys, machine time. The mouse is where that shows first, but the method — CAD, PCB, materials and prototyping under one roof — is the actual product.

how the studio runs

four rules that
survived revisions

Each one costs us something in convenience and buys back honesty.

01 / money

funded by work

Non-seeded so far: the bank account is fed by engineering services, so every hour spent on the X40G has to earn its place. That is now opening up to pre-seed and seed partners who understand hardware.

02 / ownership

in-house or it does not ship

CAD, PCB, materials and prototyping stay in the same room. Hand-offs are where weight and honesty both get lost.

03 / bench

parts before decks

Every revision ends in a physical part with measured numbers. A rendering is a guess with better lighting.

04 / channel

one open channel

Email and phone, answered by the people who design the parts — no funnel, no sales team in between.

connect

say it straight

Briefs, partnerships, testers — the channel is open and the reply is real.
