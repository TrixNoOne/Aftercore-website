privacy // your data

the
small print

No accounts, no forms, no checkout, no surprises. One stored preference and analytics you have to accept — here it is, in full.

last updated 13 october 2026

what this site stores

One small value, saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded by it and nothing else is written.

  • your analytics choice — granted or declined, so the notice stops asking and analytics stay off if you declined

There is no account, no theme switcher and no other preference stored on this site.

There is no contact form, no newsletter, no account and no checkout: everything on this site happens over email or phone, on your own mail client and carrier.

analytics

Every page carries a Google Analytics 4 tag, but it runs in Google Consent Mode with storage denied by default. Until you accept, it stores no cookies and no identifier on your device.

  • before you accept — storage denied: no cookies, no client identifier, no advertising features
  • after you accept — an identifier is stored so repeat visits can be counted
  • what is measured — pages visited and rough time on site, device type, viewport and browser, and the referring page

Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation stay denied whether or not you accept. We do not use advertising features and do not combine analytics with personal identifiers. Google processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Declining keeps analytics storage denied — now and later. You can change a previous choice at any time through the cookies link in the footer of every page.

third parties

  • hosting — the site is served as static files; the host only sees standard, short-lived request logs
  • fonts — typefaces are hosted locally with the site; no CDN request, no font-tracking
  • Google — the tag is on every page but keeps storage denied until you accept, for the analytics above
  • LinkedIn — only when you click the link; we set no trackers for it
  • Discord — the server widget in the footer is served by discord.com on every page and sets its own cookies when it loads; we do not see or store anything it collects

No payment processors, no ad networks, no fingerprinting scripts exist on these pages.

your data, your rights

Because the site holds almost nothing, exercising your rights is quick: to see it, change it or delete it, write or call and it is done in one reply — no portal, no ticket number.

updates

If this site changes what it stores, this page changes first and the date above moves. Significant changes get a line in the cookie notice. How you may use this site is covered by the terms of use.
