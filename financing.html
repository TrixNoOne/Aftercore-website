the build

AfterCore is independent and funded by client work. To put the X40G into production we are opening a pre-seed / seed conversation — and weighing a crowdfunding campaign of our own for the launch.

the round

one round,
one part

The engineering is ours and it is most of the way there. What the bench cannot buy is the second half of the job: the tooling, the certificates and the first real batch.

01 / compliance

certification

Wireless, EMC and safety approvals for a product that is sold, not just shown. Testing and paperwork paid before there is revenue.

02 / pilot

a pilot run

Enough units to put in real hands, find the failure that only appears in batch, and fix it before volume.

03 / team

a second pair of hands

Room to bring the next designer in rather than moving at the speed of one calendar.

// the shape of it

  • stage — pre-seed / seed, first outside capital; the studio stays independent
  • instrument — equity or a convertible, whatever the partner already uses
  • use of funds — tooling, certification, pilot run, one hire, in that order
  • what you get — the model, the board file, the measured weights and the failures
  • reporting — a written update when something is measured, not a monthly fiction

Nothing on this page is an offer to invest or to buy anything, or a solicitation of any kind. Figures live in the deck, under an NDA where needed.

where we are

honest
status

A financing page that hides the stage is not worth reading. Here is the line between what runs on the bench today and what does not exist yet.

// on the bench today

  • the X40G prototype — board designed and routed in house, FLARE sensing on the bench
  • mechanical cad, tolerance stacks and the thin-wall shell geometry
  • the prototyping loop: design, build, measure, revise, in weeks
  • four disciplines under one roof — no outsourcing, no translation loss
  • a client-work base that pays for the lab while this is built

// not there yet

  • production tooling — the reason this page exists
  • type approval and wireless certification
  • a pilot run, and with it a real unit cost
  • revenue of any kind, and a ship date we would sign our names to
  • any outside investor — the studio is still non-seeded
