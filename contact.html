backing aftercore

raising to
reach the line

We are looking for pre-seed and seed financing — capital that understands hardware and is happy to fund tooling, alloy and certification before volume. For the launch itself, we are weighing a crowdfunding campaign of our own.

01 / equity

pre-seed & seed

Conversations with investors who have shipped physical product before. Tell us what you back and at what stage, and we will send the deck, the models and the build log.

02 / crowd

a future campaign

A Kickstarter-style launch for the X40G is on the table for when the design is frozen. Tell us and we will write to you when it opens.

03 / where it goes

into the tooling

Money buys what the bench cannot: production tooling, certification, a pilot run and the parts that prove the 40 g before volume.

04 / either way

the same honesty

Whether it is a cheque or a contribution, you get the numbers, the failures and the revisions — the same as everyone we work with.

financing & press kit

before you write

what makes
a good brief

Four lines are enough. If you have numbers, send numbers — they shorten the first reply.

01

the budget

Target mass, target cost, or both. Even a rough range tells us if we are the right studio.

02

the constraint

The one requirement that keeps failing elsewhere — tolerance, feel, drop rating, finish.

03

the volume

One prototype, a pilot run or a production line. It changes the process, not the honesty.

04

the date

When the part has to exist. We will tell you quickly if it is not realistic.
